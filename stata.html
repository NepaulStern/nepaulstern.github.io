The Nepaul-Stern Model in Stata

Coming soon

A Stata interface to the same core is planned.

The Nepaul-Stern Model of Attitude Stability and Constraint is for panel surveys that ask the same people the same Likert items more than once (strongly agree to strongly disagree, liberal–conservative, party identification), often several attitudes together, with waves that may be unevenly spaced. Until the Stata interface is ready, the same model can be estimated from R or Python.

Nepaul and Stern (2026) introduce the model and discuss the General Social Survey estimates.

Nepaul, Alecia, and Steven Stern. 2026. “A Dynamic Discrete Choice Approach to Attitude Stability and Constraint.” Political Analysis 34: 490–509. https://doi.org/10.1017/pan.2026.10034.

Replication data: Nepaul, Alecia, and Steven Stern. 2026. “Replication Data for: A Dynamic Discrete Choice Approach to Attitude Stability and Constraint.” Harvard Dataverse. https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/DUCHYS.
